# AI Acknowledgement

TileO recognizes that **AI-assisted tools and large language models (LLMs)** may support software engineering and documentation work. Enygma acknowledges their use as assistants in portions of the TileO research, technical exploration and documentation workflow.

## Scope of assistance

AI-assisted work may include exploring implementation alternatives, explaining library APIs, reviewing or proposing code, improving technical writing, organizing documentation, discussing test approaches and identifying issues for subsequent investigation. This acknowledgement does **not** claim that a particular AI product independently designed, implemented, validated or authored TileO, nor does it establish a verified tool-by-tool history of every code change.

## Human direction and responsibility

- Product vision, scope, architectural choices, acceptance decisions and repository governance are directed by Enygma and the responsible human contributors.
- Suggestions, drafts and generated code must be checked for correctness, licensing, security and suitability before incorporation. AI-generated assertions do not replace reproducible tests or license review.
- Dataset origination, CC BY 4.0 publication decisions and third-party rights are governed by actual provenance and human authorization; AI outputs do not create those rights.
- AI systems are **not** founding-team members, Git commit authors, human contributors, license grantors or copyright owners merely because they assisted a workflow.

## Relationship to founding recognition and licenses

The historic founding contributions of **Erick Karya, Hanafi and Dony** are independently recorded in [FOUNDING_TEAM.md](FOUNDING_TEAM.md). AI assistance does not displace their recognition and does not change who actually wrote a particular commit. Original Community source remains under [AGPL-3.0-only](LICENSE) where declared; dataset inputs and third-party components retain their separately documented licenses.

Contributors should be transparent about **material AI-assisted changes** when submitting pull requests, particularly when AI-suggested code depends on third-party works, introduces a new dependency or touches security-sensitive logic. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [third-party licensing records](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

This acknowledgement documents responsible use; it is **not** a certification that every AI-assisted suggestion has passed independent verification.
