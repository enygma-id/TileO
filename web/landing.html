TileO turns GeoTIFF imagery, elevation models, and vector sources into MBTiles, PMTiles, COG, and terrain products, on one host, with one queue, and none of your data leaving the building.

Raster & vector pipelines

GDAL normalizes COG and non-COG GeoTIFFs while Tippecanoe packs vector features into MBTiles. Queued jobs handle very large inputs.

Elevation & terrain outputs

DEM, DSM, and DTM sources become elevation COGs, Terrain-RGB or Terrarium tiles, and 3D-ready glTF models.

Your storage stays yours

Read straight from your own S3-compatible storage with presigned URLs or sealed bring-your-own keys. Cross-account IAM roles are available for Enterprise editions.

Lightweight by design

One SQLite database, one worker, one host. No Restate, Redis, or object storage required.
