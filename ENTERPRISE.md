# TileO Enterprise Edition and Commercial Licensing

TileO follows two distinct licensing paths.

## 1. Community Edition

The canonical public TileO source code is licensed under **GNU Affero General Public License, Version 3 only (`AGPL-3.0-only`)**, except for clearly identified third-party components.

The AGPL is the open-source license for the public project. Its rights and obligations apply according to the license text in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## 2. Enterprise Edition

**PT Enygma Solusi Negeri** may offer a separate **TileO Enterprise Edition** and/or a separate commercial license for TileO code over which Enygma owns or has sufficient licensing rights.

Enterprise licensing is intended for organizations that require commercial terms different from the AGPL path, for example in relation to proprietary deployment, redistribution, contractual support, warranties, indemnities, integration work, private extensions, service levels, or other negotiated enterprise requirements.

The precise scope of an Enterprise Edition is defined by the applicable commercial agreement. This document does not promise that any particular feature is Enterprise-only or included in a particular commercial package.

## 3. No automatic commercial grant

This file is an explanation of the licensing model. It is **not** itself an Enterprise license grant.

A commercial/Enterprise license exists only when granted by PT Enygma Solusi Negeri under a separate written agreement or other explicit licensing instrument.

## 4. Open-source rights remain intact

A recipient who lawfully receives a TileO version under AGPL-3.0-only keeps the rights granted by that license for that version.

A later Enterprise offering, change in business model, or separate commercial agreement does not retroactively revoke an existing AGPL grant.

## 5. Contributor rights and relicensing authority

Enygma may commercially license only material for which it holds sufficient legal rights.

For external contributions, the project uses the contributor-rights process in [CLA.md](CLA.md) so accepted contributions can remain available under the Community license while also allowing Enygma, where permitted by the contributor agreement, to license those contributions under separate commercial terms.

Contributors retain the rights stated in the applicable contributor agreement; historical authorship must remain preserved.

## 6. Third-party software

Third-party dependencies are **not** relicensed by Enygma merely because they are used by TileO.

Enterprise distributions must independently comply with all applicable third-party licenses, notices, source-offer requirements, attribution requirements, binary-linking conditions, and other obligations.

See [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## 7. Trademarks and branding

Neither the Community license nor a generic reference to Enterprise Edition grants trademark rights. Rights to use Enygma or TileO branding in a commercial relationship must be addressed separately where required.

## 8. Commercial inquiries

Commercial licensing is handled by **PT Enygma Solusi Negeri** through Enygma's official business channels.


## Actual rights and distributed dependencies

An AGPL license does not itself establish that Enygma owns external code; agreements, applicable contributor grants, employment/contract rights and source provenance must be verified before a separate commercial grant. The Clean 4 npm migration is promising but native binary compliance and actual release licenses remain independent conditions.
