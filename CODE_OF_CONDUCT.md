# TileO Code of Conduct

TileO is intended to support professional, technically rigorous, and constructive collaboration.

## Expected conduct

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas, code, evidence, and outcomes;
- give credit accurately and preserve authorship/provenance;
- avoid harassment, intimidation, discrimination, personal attacks, and deliberate humiliation;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- avoid publishing another person's private information without permission;
- follow security and confidentiality requirements; and
- accept maintainer decisions made in good faith under project governance, while remaining free to document a technical disagreement respectfully.

## Unacceptable conduct

Examples include:

- harassment or targeted abuse;
- threats or incitement of harm;
- discriminatory insults;
- deliberate misattribution or erasure of contribution history;
- knowingly submitting code without the right to contribute it;
- exposing credentials, private data, or embargoed vulnerability details; and
- repeated disruption of technical discussion after moderation requests.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in official TileO project spaces and when a participant is representing the project in a public or professional context.

## Enforcement

Reports should be sent privately to the Project Steward through an official Enygma channel. Do not open a public issue for a conduct report involving private or sensitive information.

The Project Steward may issue a warning, limit participation, remove repository permissions, suspend maintainer authority, or take other proportionate action.

Enforcement actions do not erase legitimate historical authorship or founding recognition.
