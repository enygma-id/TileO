# TileO Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

> **Important:** This repository document defines the intended contributor-rights model for TileO. PT Enygma Solusi Negeri may require a separate electronic or signed acceptance record before merging a contribution. Organizations should have their legal counsel review the agreement before contributing on behalf of employees or contractors.

This is a proposed contributor-rights agreement for adoption; **a repository document alone is not signed consent**. This Contributor License Agreement (the **Agreement**) is between **PT Enygma Solusi Negeri** (**Enygma**) and the person or legal entity submitting a Contribution (**You**).

The purpose of this Agreement is to preserve contributor ownership while giving the TileO project sufficient rights to distribute accepted Contributions under the project's open-source license and, where Enygma chooses, under separate commercial terms.

## 1. Definitions

**Contribution** means any original work of authorship, modification, addition, documentation, test, design, or other material intentionally submitted by You for inclusion in TileO through a channel designated for project contributions.

**Submit** means any intentional electronic or written communication to the TileO project for the purpose of inclusion in the project, including a pull request, patch, or other contribution mechanism, but excluding material conspicuously marked as **Not a Contribution**.

## 2. Ownership

You retain ownership of Your Contribution. This Agreement is a license grant, not an assignment of copyright ownership.

## 3. Copyright license grant

Subject to the terms of this Agreement, You grant Enygma a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, irrevocable, royalty-free copyright license to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, use, make available, sublicense, and distribute Your Contribution and derivative works of it.

This grant includes the right for Enygma to license an accepted Contribution:

1. as part of the canonical public TileO project under **AGPL-3.0-only**; and
2. under one or more separate proprietary, commercial, or Enterprise licenses where Enygma elects to do so.

This commercial relicensing permission does not prevent You from using or licensing Your own original Contribution elsewhere, subject to rights You may have granted to others.

## 4. Patent license grant

To the extent You can legally grant such rights, You grant Enygma and recipients of TileO a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free patent license under patent claims controlled by You that are necessarily infringed by Your Contribution alone or by its combination with the TileO work to which it was submitted, to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import, and otherwise transfer the Contribution as part of TileO.

This patent grant does not extend to claims that would be infringed only because of later modifications or combinations not contemplated by Your Contribution.

## 5. Right to grant

You represent that You are legally entitled to grant the rights in this Agreement.

If an employer, client, university, funding body, or other entity may own or control rights in Your Contribution, You represent that You have obtained all authorization necessary to submit the Contribution under this Agreement, or that the rights-holder has separately authorized the Contribution.

## 6. Original work and third-party material

You represent that Your Contribution is Your original work except for third-party material that is clearly identified and legally permitted for inclusion.

You must disclose material third-party licensing, attribution, patent, trademark, dataset, standard-text, or other restrictions known to You that could affect distribution of the Contribution.

You must not submit confidential information, trade secrets, copied proprietary code, or material obtained under terms incompatible with the intended project distribution.

## 7. Open-source publication

You understand that accepted Contributions may be made publicly available under **AGPL-3.0-only** as part of TileO and may be copied, modified, and redistributed by third parties under that license.

## 8. Commercial licensing

You understand and agree that the license granted in Section 3 permits Enygma to offer accepted Contributions under a separate commercial or Enterprise license in addition to the AGPL distribution.

Such commercial licensing by Enygma does not transfer ownership of Your Contribution to Enygma and does not revoke an AGPL license already granted to recipients of an AGPL release.

## 9. No obligation to use or support

Enygma is not required to accept, use, distribute, maintain, or commercially license any Contribution.

Unless separately agreed, You are not required to provide support for Your Contribution.

## 10. Disclaimer

Except where You intentionally provide a separate written warranty, Your Contribution is provided without warranties or conditions beyond those that cannot legally be excluded.

## 11. Acceptance record

The Project Steward may use a repository CLA workflow, signed document, electronic acceptance system, or other auditable mechanism to record acceptance of this Agreement.

A pull-request checkbox alone may be treated as an acknowledgement but does not prevent the Project Steward from requiring an additional acceptance record before merge.

## 12. Corporate contributions

A person submitting on behalf of a legal entity must be authorized to grant the rights described in this Agreement for that entity's Contributions. Enygma may require a separate corporate CLA or confirmation of authority.
